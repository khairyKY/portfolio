A full life-management PWA replacing Akiflow: calendar time-blocking, task management with drag-and-drop reordering, journal, reading library, Pomodoro timer, and stopwatch. Custom botanical/garden design system. Built with React 19 + TypeScript, Supabase for auth and data, FullCalendar for scheduling, and deployed on Cloudflare Workers.
